      About

      Oxford Break

      Oxford's real streets and buildings, taken from OpenStreetMap and rebuilt as a stylised 3D city you can knock down. Two modes: bombing against the clock, and a helicopter run with a door gunner.

      Modelled on Campus Break by Peerly, the HKU original. This Oxford version is an independent remake with its own code and art.

      Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). 3D with three.js. Made by Jintian.
